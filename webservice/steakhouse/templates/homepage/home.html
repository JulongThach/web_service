About Us

Learn More About Us

Welcome to Oxly Steakhouse, Phnom Penh’s hidden gem for steak lovers and families alike. At Oxly, we pride ourselves on offering a dining experience that combines delicious meals, a warm ambiance, and exceptional value for money.

  • Our dishes are thoughtfully crafted with generous portions that evoke the comforting feeling of home-cooked meals. Whether you’re savoring our perfectly grilled steaks or indulging in hearty sides, every bite reflects our commitment to quality and care.
  • Set in a charming and relaxed atmosphere, Oxly Steakhouse is the perfect spot for family gatherings, casual dinners, or special celebrations. We believe great food should be accessible to all, and our menu ensures you enjoy premium flavors without stretching your budget.

Join us at Oxly Steakhouse, where every meal feels like it’s made with love—because it truly is.
